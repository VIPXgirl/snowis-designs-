Frequently Asked Questions

I hope you'll find the answers you need here. If you have any questions about ordering or customization, or just need a little extra guidance, feel free to get in touch anytime :)

⏱️ How long will my order take? +

Most commissions are completed within 2–7 days, depending on the complexity of the artwork and my current queue. Before accepting your order, I’ll let you know the estimated completion time, so you’ll know what to expect. If I expect your commission to take longer, I’ll let you know in advance! :)

🎨 Can you draw in different styles? +

I’m happy to work with a variety of art styles and am always open to new ideas. Feel free to send me references or examples of the style you’re looking for, and I’ll let you know if it’s something I can create! :)

🖼️ Do I need to provide references? +

References are always appreciated! They help me better understand your vision and create artwork that matches what you have in mind. Feel free to provide as many details or examples as you’d like—the more information you share, the better I can bring your idea to life! :)

✏️ Can I request changes? +

Of course! Minor revisions are included with every commission. They ensure the final artwork meets your expectations. If you have any adjustments or changes in mind, simply let me know, and I’ll be happy to make them! :)

📁 What will I receive? +

You’ll receive your completed artwork as a high-quality digital file, typically in PNG format. If you require a different file format, please let me know before your commission is completed, and I’ll be happy to accommodate your request where possible. ;)

💬 Still have a question? +
